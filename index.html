Privacy Policy: Run Walk Timer

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This policy explains what the Android app Run Walk Timer ("the app") does with your information. The app is made by Siwat ("we"). Contact: siwatarm2013@gmail.com.

Summary

  • The app works without an account, sign-in or GPS.
  • Your workouts, custom workouts and settings stay on your phone. We do not receive them.
  • Free users see banner ads from Google AdMob on the setup and history screens only. AdMob may collect device information to show and measure ads.
  • If you buy Pro, Google Play handles the payment. We never see your card details.

Information stored on your device

The app stores the following only on your phone:

  • Custom workouts you create (name, run and walk times, repeats, warm-up and cool-down)
  • Workout history (date, workout name, planned and completed time, whether you finished)
  • Settings (voice, vibration and cue options, and whether you own Pro)

We cannot see or access this data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. The app has no cloud backup of this data.

Third parties

Google AdMob (free version only). To show and measure banner ads, the AdMob SDK may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device and app information, and ad interaction data. Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads and policies.google.com/privacy. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your phone's settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads). Where the law requires it, the app asks for your consent before ads are shown, and you can change your choice later under Settings › Privacy choices in the app.

Google Play Billing (Pro purchase). When you buy Pro, Google Play processes the purchase under Google's terms. The app only receives confirmation that the purchase is valid, so it can unlock Pro. See policies.google.com/privacy.

Pro removes ads, so the app does not start AdMob for Pro users.

Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to show the ongoing workout notification with pause, skip and stop.
  • Foreground service (media playback): to keep the timer and voice cues running while the screen is locked.
  • Wake lock: to keep the timer accurate while the screen is off during a workout.
  • Vibration: for cue vibrations.
  • Internet and network state: added by the AdMob and Google Play Billing libraries and used only by them.

The app does not request location, contacts, camera, microphone or your files. If you choose to follow the in-app battery guide, the app only opens your phone's own settings; it does not change them itself.

Children

The app is a general-audience fitness tool and is not directed at children under 13.

Data security and retention

Data on your device is protected by your phone's own security. We do not keep any copy of it. Data processed by Google AdMob and Google Play is kept according to Google's policies.

Your choices

  • Delete your data: clear the app's storage or uninstall the app.
  • Stop ads: buy Pro, or limit ad personalization in your phone settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and publish the new version at this address.
